Current operational blockers

Three items are preventing the next handoff:

Before the next handoff

The intake coordinator can see that the purchase contract is in, but the file should not move to engagement or matter opening yet. The next work is owned: attorney conflict review, firm acceptance decision, and onboarding-document review.

LawIntake is showing the work state and handoff blockers—not deciding whether the firm may act or whether the matter is legally complete.
