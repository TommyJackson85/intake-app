Intake and document tracker

A structured tracker separates what has been requested, received, reviewed, and still needs follow-up. Requirements vary by firm, transaction, and professional judgment.

Illustrative intake and document tracker with status, ownership, and next actions
Item Status Requested Received Owner Next action
Client contact details ✓ Complete — Yes Intake coordinator Ready
Transaction summary ✓ Complete — Yes Intake coordinator Ready
Purchase contract ✓ Received Yes Yes Paralegal Review details
Identification documents ! Needs review Yes Yes Intake coordinator Confirm completeness
Association contact details ✓ Complete Yes Yes Transaction coordinator Ready
Estoppel request → In progress Yes No Transaction coordinator Follow up
Condo questionnaire ! Missing Yes No Transaction coordinator Request from association
Association documents ✓ Received Yes Yes Paralegal Review
Reserve-related information ! Needs review Yes Yes Reviewer Flag for internal review
Inspection/SIRS-related item ? Review applicability Depends Depends Reviewer Confirm applicability

This is an operational example. Teams should adapt the items, review steps, and ownership rules to their own matter types and procedures.

Ownership and key dates

The goal is to avoid unclear “pending” work by showing the next action, its owner, and whether it affects the handoff.

Illustrative ownership and timing for key intake follow-up work items
Work item Current status Assigned owner Next step Timing

Purchase contract review ✓ Ready for review Paralegal Review received contract Next internal review

Condo diligence snapshot

For applicable condominium or association-related matters, a team may need a clearer way to track information, documents, review status, and outstanding questions alongside the main intake workflow.

Illustrative condo diligence snapshot with review areas, status, and visible outcomes

From first contact to file-ready handoff

  1. 1

    Client contact

    Capture the initial enquiry and basic details.

  2. 2

    Information captured

    Record matter context and relevant contacts.

  3. 3

    Documents requested

    Make document requests visible and trackable.

  4. 4

    Completeness review

    See what has arrived and what remains incomplete.

  5. 5

    Follow-up and exceptions

    Assign ownership for missing items or approved exceptions.

  6. 6

    File-ready handoff

    Move the matter forward with a clear status and next owner.

What this demonstrates

  • One visible place for intake context, document status, and outstanding work.
  • Clear ownership for follow-ups rather than vague “pending” tasks.
  • A structured view of readiness before a matter is handed to the next stage.
  • A way to keep applicable condo-related review items visible without turning the workflow into a generic CRM.
