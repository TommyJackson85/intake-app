From intake to matter handoff

    Enquiry received

    Capture the new residential purchase enquiry.

  2. 2

    Minimum screening info

    Collect enough detail for initial internal screening.

  3. 3

    Conflict details submitted

    Send party names for firm conflict review.

  4. 4

    Firm reviews

    Attorney or intake team decides whether to proceed.

  5. 5

    Engagement requested

    Send onboarding / engagement items after acceptance.

  6. 6

    Details and documents

    Collect client details, supporting docs, and firm-configured matter items.

  7. 7

    Open or hand off

    Open the matter or hand off when the responsible firm reviewer marks the configured items ready.

What this demonstrates

  • Client and matter intake context in one clearer operational view.
  • Document collection, follow-ups, and outstanding items with visible ownership.
  • Open items and readiness signals before the next handoff—not a claim of legal completeness.
  • A configurable real-estate law-firm workflow that can adapt stages, roles, and matter-specific checklists to the firm’s process.
