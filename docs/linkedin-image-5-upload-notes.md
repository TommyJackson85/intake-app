## Recommended post copy

Email is useful for communicating with a client, but it is a weak place to run an intake workflow.

In real-estate transactions, information and documents can arrive across multiple email threads, attachments, follow-ups, and team members. The issue is often not simply that a document is missing.

It is that the team cannot easily see what is missing, who owns the next follow-up, or whether an outstanding item prevents the file from moving forward.

I put together a short practical guide on the common failure points and a simple file-ready workflow:

https://tommyjackson85.github.io/intake-app/insights/why-email-client-intake-causes-transaction-delays/
