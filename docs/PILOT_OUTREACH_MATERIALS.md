## 1. Core positioning

### One-sentence value proposition (< 30 words)

LawIntake helps Florida real-estate teams classify vague “property-tax” intakes into assessment/VAB, buyer tax-estimate, or tax-deed tracks and organize facts for attorney review.

### 30-second spoken explanation

“Clients often say they have a property-tax problem, but that can mean assessment or VAB work, a buyer relying on a seller’s tax bill, or a tax-deed or surplus-notice matter. Our demo shows how intake can separate those tracks, capture notices and dates with verification status, and suggest documents for staff to request—without the software giving legal advice or calculating deadlines. Everything in the demo uses fictional data. I’m looking for Florida firms to tell me whether this would reduce misrouting and back-and-forth.”

### 90-second spoken explanation

“I’m validating a workflow for Florida real-estate law firms—not a tax calculator or filing tool. When a new matter comes in as a vague property-tax issue, staff may not know whether it belongs with assessment/VAB, closing/investor tax-estimate review, or tax-deed and surplus-notice work. The demo walks through three fictional scenarios: an assessment/TRIM/VAB-style intake, an investor purchase where the seller’s current tax bill may not be a safe estimate, and a former owner or heir reporting a Clerk surplus-related notice. In each path you see issue classification, a compact Matter Overview panel, date tracking with client-reported, documented, firm-verified, and unknown states, and staff-controlled suggested document requests—staff review and decide; nothing auto-sends. The product does not decide appeal rights, entitlement, lien priority, or legal deadlines, and it does not file or contact agencies. I’m seeking short discovery calls and a few design partners for a limited fictional/demo pilot to see if this reduces intake confusion and document chasing.”

### Audience variations

**Property-tax / VAB lawyers**  
“When new clients arrive with TRIM or assessment concerns, intake often lacks the notice facts, petition status, and documents you need before attorney review. The demo shows a fictional Assessment/VAB track that organizes those intake facts, date verification states, and staff-suggested document requests—without claiming appeal validity or calculating filing deadlines.”

**Florida closing / real-estate transaction firms**  
“Investor and flipper buyers often treat the seller’s current tax bill as their future estimate. The demo shows a fictional buyer tax-estimate track that captures buyer use, seller-homestead facts, and tax-document status so closers and attorneys can flag review—without predicting reassessment or tax amounts.”

**Tax-deed / title / probate / surplus-adjacent practices**  
“Former owners and heirs often arrive with a Clerk notice and incomplete ownership records. The demo shows a fictional tax-deed/surplus track that separates that work from VAB and ordinary closings, tracks recorded dates with firm-verification wording, and suggests missing title or heirship documents for staff to request—without determining surplus entitlement or lien priority.”

### What the product is not

LawIntake (this demo feature) is **not**: legal or tax advice; a VAB/tax appeal engine; a tax, reassessment, surplus, or lien calculator; a deadline confirmation system; a filing or payment service; an auto-email/reminder product; or a claim of production-ready legal software. Demo data is fictional.

## 2. Ideal pilot profile

### Best-fit firm size and practice

- Small to mid-size Florida firms (roughly solo–~25 lawyers) with real-estate transactional work **and/or** property-tax/VAB, title, tax-deed, probate/heirship, or litigation adjacent to notices.
- Closing/title teams that regularly chase tax notices, lien/title docs, and time-sensitive client facts.
- Firms that already feel intake misrouting between transaction, property-tax, title, probate, and litigation teams (per Walkthrough 6 discovery framing).

### Best internal users

| Role | Why they matter |
|------|-----------------|
| Intake coordinator | First to hear vague “property-tax” language |
| Paralegal / closing coordinator | Chases notices, documents, date verification |
| Associate | Triages facts before partner review |
| Partner / practice lead | Owns routing standards and pilot yes/no |

### Pain signals (prioritize)

- Staff ask “is this VAB, closing, or tax-deed?” on new leads.
- Documents and notice dates are incomplete before attorney review.
- Investor/LLC buyers rely on seller tax bills without a structured review flag.
- Former-owner/heir matters arrive with Clerk notices and missing authority docs.
- Existing checklists live in email/Word and do not survive handoff.

### Disqualifiers / poor fit

- Wants AI legal advice, automated appeals, guaranteed deadlines, or surplus recovery promises.
- Requires Clio/MyCase integrations or production security certifications before any conversation.
- No Florida real-estate or notice-related work at all.
- Only interested in free legal work or a fixed launch commitment.
- Cannot spare ~20 minutes and will not look at fictional demo data.

### Scoring rubric (0–10) — prioritize discovery if ≥ 7

| Points | Criterion |
|--------|-----------|
| 0–2 | Florida RE / title / PT / tax-deed / probate adjacency (0 = none, 2 = core practice) |
| 0–2 | Evidence of intake confusion or document chasing (0 = none, 2 = frequent) |
| 0–2 | Has intake + paralegal/coordinator capacity to give feedback |
| 0–2 | Open to fictional/demo-first pilot (not integration-first) |
| 0–1 | Decision-maker or champion on the call |
| 0–1 | Willing to introduce another Florida firm if useful |

**Rule:** Score ≥ 7 → schedule discovery; 5–6 → nurture/follow-up; ≤ 4 → polite decline or short feedback-only.

## 5. Discovery call plan (20 minutes)

| Time | Focus |
|------|--------|
| 0–2 min | Context + permission: feedback call; fictional demo; not legal advice; not a sales close |
| 2–5 min | Learn current intake: who answers the phone / form; how “property-tax” is labeled today |
| 5–12 min | Demo **one** relevant scenario from `/demo` staff matter (`FL-2026-007`, `008`, or `009`) |
| 12–17 min | Test concept: would this change handoff? what missing? |
| 17–20 min | Pilot fit + next step (or graceful no) |

### 15 open-ended discovery questions

1. How do new matters that mention “property tax,” TRIM, assessment, tax certificate, or tax deed enter the firm today?  
2. Who first decides whether that matter is transactional, property-tax, title, probate, or litigation?  
3. How often is that first classification wrong or incomplete?  
4. What does a “good enough for attorney review” packet look like?  
5. Which documents are chased most often before review?  
6. Which dates are most often missing, wrong, or unverified?  
7. How do you record whether a date was client-reported vs pulled from a notice vs firm-verified?  
8. For investor/LLC buyers, how do you handle reliance on the seller’s current tax bill?  
9. For former owners/heirs, what ownership or authority documents stall the file?  
10. Where do checklists live today (email, Word, PMS tasks, memory)?  
11. What happens when two issue types might both apply?  
12. What would make another system intolerable even if the workflow idea is good?  
13. What would “less back-and-forth” look like in a real week?  
14. Who would own a two-to-four-week feedback pilot internally?  
15. If this helped, who else in Florida should I learn from?

### 5 “show me how you currently do this” prompts

1. Walk me through the last vague “property-tax” intake from first contact to attorney eyes.  
2. Show (or describe) the checklist or task list you use for TRIM/assessment facts.  
3. How do you mark that a notice date still needs firm verification?  
4. How does a closer flag buyer tax-estimate concern without calculating a new tax bill?  
5. How is a Clerk surplus-notice matter kept separate from a purchase file?

### 5 questions — urgent pain vs polite interest

1. In the last 30 days, how many matters hit this confusion or chase pattern?  
2. What did the last misfire cost in time or rework (even roughly)?  
3. Is someone already tasked with fixing intake routing this quarter?  
4. If we did nothing, what continues as-is?  
5. Would you rearrange a closing-week calendar for a second conversation, or only if it’s easy?

### 5 questions — pilot partner potential

1. Can you work from fictional/demo matters first?  
2. Who gives feedback weekly for 2–4 weeks?  
3. What success would make the pilot “worth it”?  
4. What are you unwilling to do (integrations, live client data, custom build)?  
5. Would you introduce one peer firm if the demo is useful?

## 6. Demo scripts (≈ 5 minutes each)

*Prefer **View sample matter** from `/demo` → Florida Property-Tax & Tax-Deed Demo Scenarios. Demo data is fictional.*

### A. Assessment / VAB path (`FL-2026-007`)

**Exact first sentence:**  
“A client says the assessment looks too high—I’m going to show how intake can treat that as an assessment/VAB track instead of a tax-deed or ordinary closing file, using fictional sample data.”

**Screens / data to show:** Scenario card → matter Overview **Property Tax & Tax Deed** (Assessment/VAB kind, TRIM received, assessed value) → Key Dates (client-reported / documented verification) → suggested documents (staff review; do not auto-create unless demonstrating control).

**Do not say:** That the appeal is valid; that a date is the legal deadline; that the firm must file; any tax savings claim.

**End question:** “Would classifying assessment/VAB facts this way reduce misrouting or document chasing before attorney review in your firm?”

### B. Investor buyer tax-estimate path (`FL-2026-008`)

**Exact first sentence:**  
“Here’s a fictional investor buyer who may be treating the seller’s current tax bill as their future estimate—I’ll show how we capture that for review without predicting taxes.”

**Screens / data to show:** Overview buyer tax-estimate track; buyer use (flipper/investor); seller homestead yes; tax bill available; firm-verified closing as internal fact; suggested docs not preselecting items marked available.

**Do not say:** Homestead will/won’t transfer; tax will increase; any reassessment amount; that the software confirmed a legal outcome.

**End question:** “For investor or LLC purchases, would a structured flag like this help closers hand off to an attorney without doing tax math in the intake tool?”

### C. Tax-deed / surplus path (`FL-2026-009`)

**Exact first sentence:**  
“This fictional former-owner/heir matter starts with a tax-deed-related Clerk notice—I’ll show how we keep it out of the VAB and ordinary closing buckets.”

**Screens / data to show:** Delinquent/tax-deed kind only; situations and client role; documented sale/notice dates; notice-stated date with **“Deadline reported or documented — firm verification required.”**; suggested title/heirship docs for staff decision.

**Do not say:** Client is entitled to funds; claim will succeed; deadline is legally controlling; lien priority is determined.

**End question:** “If a Clerk surplus-related notice arrived tomorrow, what would you need organized before an attorney looks at it—and does this framing help?”

## 7. Pilot offer (restrained)

**Invitation draft**

We’re inviting a small number of Florida real-estate practices to a **2–4 week design-partner pilot** focused on the Property-Tax & Tax-Deed workflow demo.

**Scope**
- Week 1: guided walkthrough of fictional scenarios on `/demo` (Assessment/VAB, investor tax-estimate, tax-deed/surplus).  
- Weeks 2–4: optional feedback on the same fictional flows; a small number of non-confidential or appropriately authorized sample matters **only if the firm is comfortable**—never required.

**What we need**
- 1–2 short feedback sessions (or written notes) on classification clarity, document suggestions, date verification wording, and routing value.  
- Honest “would not use” feedback.

**What the firm receives**
- Structured walkthrough and a simple scorecard.  
- A voice in prioritization (templates vs routing rules, etc.).  
- No obligation to buy, integrate, or continue.

**What we will not ask**
- Live confidential client data by default.  
- Production rollout, integrations, security certification proofs, or bespoke development as a condition of the pilot.  
- Endorsements or case-result claims.

**Lightweight success definition**  
At least two of: (1) clearer intake classification than current practice, (2) fewer missing-doc surprises before attorney review in the demo workflow, (3) willingness to introduce one peer firm or continue a second feedback cycle.
