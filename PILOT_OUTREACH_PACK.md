# Pilot Outreach Pack

**Purpose:** Recruit the first **five** Florida real-estate legal professionals for 20-minute discovery sessions using `PILOT_SESSION_RUNBOOK.md`.  
**Product surface:** Early interactive demo at https://intake-app-dun.vercel.app/demo  
**Tone:** Research first — not a sales close. Feedback shapes the workflow.

This pack is **copy only**. It does not change application behaviour.

---

# Positioning statement

LawIntake is an early Florida real-estate intake and matter-workflow demo for closing teams. It helps turn a property enquiry into a structured, reviewable file—documents, follow-ups, condo diligence, and internal hand-offs—so staff can see what is missing before closing work stalls. It does not give legal advice or replace lawyer judgement.

*(49 words)*

# Who to contact

## Highest priority (first five conversations)

| Priority | Profile | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Florida closing / real-estate transactional lawyer | Owns file standards and can say whether structured intake beats email/drive workarounds |
| 2 | Real-estate paralegal or closing coordinator | Lives in document chasing, association records, and day-to-day follow-up |
| 3 | Transaction coordinator / intake coordinator | First to open files and route hand-offs |
| 4 | Legal-operations staff in a Florida RE practice | Cares about readiness, status visibility, and repeatable process |
| 5 | Closing/title attorney who also sees entity buyers or condo files | Can react to FinCEN/AML review surfaces and condo diligence without treating them as filing decisions |

**Ideal signals:** Handles inbound property matters, client follow-up, document collection, condo/association records, closing workflows, or matter hand-offs in a solo–~25 lawyer Florida practice.

## Not a priority yet

| Profile | Why defer |
|---|---|
| Firms shopping for a full practice-management / CRM replacement | Out of scope for this demo |
| Teams that only want billing, accounting, or generic e-signature | Not what `/demo` demonstrates |
| Buyers seeking automated legal advice, deadline engines, or “AI decisions” | Misaligned with product boundaries |
| Pure litigation counsel with no intake/closing operations | Will not exercise the primary workflow |
| Anyone requiring production integrations or security certifications before a research chat | Too early for that gate |

# Objection responses

## “We already use Clio / MyCase / PracticePanther / another system.”

“That’s common—and useful context. I’m not asking you to replace your case-management system. I’m researching whether a more structured **intake-to-file-ready** path (documents, follow-ups, hand-offs) would still help beside what you already use. Twenty minutes is plenty to see if the problem is even real for your team.”

## “We do not handle condo matters.”

“Understood. The core conversation is still about turning an enquiry into a reviewable file and chasing missing documents. Condo diligence is only one sample path in the demo; we can stay on the general intake and matter-overview flow.”

## “We do not handle property-tax/VAB matters.”

“That’s fine. Assessment/VAB in the demo is an **optional** notice-triage example, not the main ask. The pilot question is whether structured intake, document follow-up, and internal hand-offs beat email and drive workarounds on ordinary closing files.”

## “I am too busy.”

“Completely fair. Would a **20-minute** slot next week work better, or is there a paralegal or transaction coordinator on your team who owns document chasing and might be a better fit for a research chat?”

## “Is this legal advice or AI making decisions?”

“Neither. LawIntake is an early **workflow and review organiser**. Statuses and dates are for staff tracking and firm verification. It does not give legal advice, decide compliance outcomes, or replace lawyer judgement.”

## “How do you handle confidential client information?”

“For these research sessions we use **fictional demo data only**—please don’t enter real client information. The interactive demo is labelled as demo mode and is meant for workflow feedback, not live client files. I’m not asking you to migrate production data or rely on unverified compliance claims.”

# Five-session recruitment plan

**Goal:** Five completed discovery sessions in about one week of focused outreach (calendar time may stretch if closings intervene).

**Target mix:** ~2 lawyers · ~2 paralegals/transaction coordinators · ~1 legal-ops or intake lead (flexible if quality conversations appear).

| Day | Activity | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | List 15–20 relevant Florida contacts from LinkedIn + referrals. Fill tracker rows (real people only). Send **8–10** personal LinkedIn connection notes. Ask **2** warm contacts for referrals. | Pipeline started |
| **Day 2** | Send follow-up messages to **accepted** connections. Send **3–5** careful cold emails where email is known and relevant. No bulk tools. | First replies |
| **Day 3** | Follow up non-responders once (LinkedIn or email). Book any “yes” into calendar with confirmation message. Keep mix of lawyer vs coordinator. | 1–2 sessions booked |
| **Day 4** | Second wave: **5–8** new connection notes + **2** referral nudges. Confirm tomorrow’s sessions; reset demo before each call. | Pipeline topped up |
| **Day 5** | Run booked sessions using `PILOT_SESSION_RUNBOOK.md`. Same-day notes. Thank-you messages within 24 hours. | Sessions underway |
| **Day 6** | Follow up soft “maybes.” Offer coordinator alternative if lawyers stay too busy. Avoid a third chase to the same person. | Fill remaining seats |
| **Day 7** | Final booking push for session five. Pause new cold outreach. Prepare comparison of notes after five completes. | Five sessions secured or clearly in diary |

**Follow-up timing:** First follow-up after ~2–3 working days; one polite close-out after ~7 days of silence. Prefer quality over volume. **Do not** use mass spam or automated LinkedIn sequences.

# Guardrails

**Do not say or imply:**

- “AI legal advice,” autonomous legal decisions, or that the product replaces lawyer judgement
- That LawIntake **calculates** authoritative legal deadlines or confirms filing timeliness
- Automated compliance, conflict clearance, AML filing determinations, or closing readiness certification
- That it **replaces** Clio/MyCase/PracticePanther or is a full practice-management platform / generic CRM
- Security, compliance, or certification claims **unless** you can point to an explicit, current statement in the repository (do not invent SOC/ISO/GDPR guarantees in outreach)
- Integrations with CMS, title, or county systems that are not demonstrated
- Customer counts, revenue, or “firms already using this in production” (this pack is for an **early demo**)
- Requests for confidential client facts, live matter data, or production credentials

**Do say:**

- Early demo / research conversation / fictional sample data
- Structured intake → reviewable file; document follow-up; internal hand-offs
- Optional condo diligence and AML/FinCEN **review surfaces**; optional Assessment/VAB **notice triage**
- Participant need not prepare; 20 minutes; not a sales call
